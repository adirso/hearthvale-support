Hearthvale Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Hearthvale is a single-player strategy game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

Information we collect

None. Hearthvale has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To let you resume a game, Hearthvale saves your current game and your settings (the name you type, number of players, rival difficulty, colour, pace, sound and haptics) on your device only. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Hearthvale collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings; these are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
